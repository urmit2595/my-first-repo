Shuriken Showdown

Best-of-three arena duel. You play Kaito; Ryn is controlled by the AI.

Move

  • A / D or ← / → move
  • W or ↑ jump
  • S or ↓ guard (blocks 75%)

Attack

  • J Palm Strike (8)
  • K Throwing Star (6)
  • J → K Spiral Burst combo (16, close range)
  • L Vortex Blast (18, costs 50 energy)

Tips

  • Jump over stars, or guard while facing them.
  • Stars cancel each other out; a Vortex goes through stars.
  • Energy fills when you deal or take damage.
  • Enter starts, P pauses.
