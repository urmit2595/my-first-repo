Append one line per request: `- [from → to] request`. The orchestrator relays and marks them done.

- [geometry → engine] The solver's walk model, which the engine must match: a walk follows `w.path()` and stops at the first ACTIVE plate after the start tile (a used once-plate is inactive). Walking off a plate does not trigger it. The goal counts only when a walk ENDS on it. A plate on an x/z rotor while Wick stands on that rotor is locked and does nothing. Tell me if the engine differs.
